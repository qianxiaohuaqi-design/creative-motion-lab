A LETTER WRITTEN IN THE SKY

Some days the sky holds every word in place. Then the wind arrives, almost too quietly to notice. A letter tilts. Another drifts. Soon the sentences loosen into pale clouds, and what once made sense begins to float away.

Even the gentlest wind can change a story.
