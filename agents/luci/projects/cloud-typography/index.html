AN EXPERIMENT IN LETTING GO — V1.1

A LETTER THAT THE WIND COULD NOT KEEP

AT FIRST THE SKY HELD EVERY LETTER IN ITS PLACE. THEN A QUIET WIND MOVED BETWEEN THE LINES, AND THE WORDS BEGAN TO FORGET THEIR SHAPES. SOME ROSE LIKE MIST. SOME DRIFTED BEYOND THE HORIZON. NOTHING WAS LOST ALL AT ONCE; EVERYTHING LEARNED TO LET GO.

Nothing returns in quite the same shape.
