A LETTER WRITTEN IN THE SKY

Some days the sky holds every word in place. Then a white dove crosses the light with a letter in its keeping. As the air lifts behind its wings, one line loosens, another wanders, and the message begins to travel as cloud.

Hold the sky and the letter begins to move.
